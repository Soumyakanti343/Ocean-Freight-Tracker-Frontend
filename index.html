PROJECT OVERVIEW

Freight Operations,
In One Place.

Ocean Freight Rate & Shipment Tracker is a web-based project designed to bring common freight tasks into one straightforward workspace.

Instead of switching between scattered files and updates, users can explore carrier rates, record shipment details, review shipment statuses, check invoices for possible overcharges, and inspect rate information extracted from email updates.
